About Ariel

A life devoted to women's wellbeing.

Olivia Ariel Mamle Donkor is a certified wellness and menopause coach, the CEO of Ariel's Haven and the founder of the Ariel Wellness Club. Through coaching, community meet-ups, retreats and honest conversations on radio and television, she helps women understand their bodies and put their wellbeing first.

A guide to menopause

Not a disease. A stage to embrace.

Previous generations often suffered in silence. Ariel's mission is to make menopause understood rather than feared, so every woman can prepare and thrive.

i.

Perimenopause

The transition years, when changes begin and symptoms first appear.

ii.

Menopause

The point at which periods have stopped for good.

iii.

Post-menopause

The years after: a season to protect your heart, bones and joy.

34+

symptoms can accompany menopause, from hot flashes, mood swings and poor sleep to joint pain and heart health.
